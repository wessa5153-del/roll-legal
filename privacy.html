4. International transfers

Google and Photon servers may be located outside your country (EU, US and other regions). Transfers rely on your explicit consent where required, on Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the providers, and on the safeguards required by applicable data-protection law (including Art. 9 of the Turkish KVKK). Voice and chat data are transmitted only for the duration of a session.

5. Microphone and voice chat

The microphone permission is optional and requested separately through the Android permission dialog. If you decline, voice chat stays off and everything else works. You can revoke it at any time in Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Roll? ▸ Permissions ▸ Microphone. Audio is live-streamed and not recorded by us. Recording other players is prohibited (see the Terms of Service).

6. Ads and personalisation

The game uses Google AdMob for rewarded and banner ads. Personalised advertising is used only with your explicit consent (required in the EEA, the UK and Türkiye); otherwise non-personalised ads are served. Ad and privacy consent is collected through Google's own consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) and your choices are managed by Google. You can reopen Google's privacy options form at any time with the "PRIVACY SETTINGS" button in the game to change or withdraw your consent. You can also change your preferences in Google Settings ▸ Ads or reset/delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

For rewarded ads, the number of ads you must watch is shown in a confirmation panel before the first ad. If you exit before completing an ad, no reward is granted.

7. Retention

  • On-device data: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud save: in your Google Play Games account until you delete it. Uninstalling the game does not delete it.
  • Photon session, chat and voice data: for the duration of the session; not retained by us.
  • Purchase records: retained by Google and for the periods required by tax and commercial law.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS). Cloud saves are stored on Google infrastructure and multiplayer data on Photon infrastructure. No transmission method is 100% secure.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent. Under the Turkish KVKK (Art. 11) you may learn whether your data is processed, request information, learn the purpose and the recipients, request correction or deletion, object to automated analysis, and claim compensation for unlawful processing. Under the CCPA/CPRA you may request information, request deletion and opt out of sale/sharing — we do not sell your data.

To exercise any right, email wessa5153@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or your local supervisory authority.

10. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. Because it contains advertising and voice chat, it is not offered as a child-directed app. In the European Union the digital age of consent varies by country between 13 and 16; if you are below the applicable age in your country, do not use the game. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided data, we delete it. Parents or guardians may contact wessa5153@gmail.com.
